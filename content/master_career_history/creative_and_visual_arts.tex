\section{Creative and Visual Arts}

\textbf{Media:} Relief printmaking (woodcut and linocut), intaglio (etching, drypoint, photo-print, and aquatint), screenprint, monoprint, monotype, and papermaking; oil and acrylic painting; graphite, pencil, charcoal, ink, marker, pastel, and mixed-media drawing; photography.

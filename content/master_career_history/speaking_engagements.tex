\section{Speaking Engagements}
\phantomsection
\label{sec:speaking_engagements}

\presentation{Surgical Scheduling Optimization and Procedure Duration Prediction}{IBM AI Hackathon}{Dec. 2020}{Presented a prototype that combined historical operating-room utilization and the current surgical wait list to optimize scheduling and improve total procedure-time estimates beyond traditional fixed-ratio methods; outlined CNN/RNN case-card prediction as a path toward a potential new offering.}
\presentation{IBM Watson Care Insights}{IBM REACH Initiative}{2019}{Delivered conceptual and hands-on training with synthetic patient data, explaining how longitudinal records, real-time HL7 feeds, and FHIR configuration support clinical-data integration and the delivery of insights within physicians' EHR workflows.}
\presentation{Machine Learning with Real-World Health Data}{Content and Data Analytics Learning Exchange}{2018}{Introduced an implementation-oriented approach to applying machine-learning algorithms to real-world health data, connecting clinical-data preparation, model development, evaluation, and interpretation.}
\presentation{Natural-Language Processing for Unstructured Data Lakes}{Innovation Research Interchange Fall Networks Conference}{Sep. 2018}{Presented a healthcare case study using natural-language processing and topic modeling to turn nearly six million semi-structured provider notes in a data lake into analyzable themes and features.}
\presentation{Hadoop Ingestion and Data-Lake Concepts}{Cross-Team Training Series}{2016}{Designed and led a seven-part series connecting hands-on Hadoop/HDFS ingestion workflows with distributed storage, transformation, validation, troubleshooting, and the use of data lakes for heterogeneous clinical data.}
\presentation{Massive Stellar Clusters in the Disk of the Milky Way Galaxy}{M.S. Physics Thesis Defense, University of Cincinnati}{Jun. 2010}{Defended research using near-infrared broad-band photometry and spectroscopy to identify and characterize two dust-obscured Galactic disk clusters and estimate their distance, age, and approximate mass.}
\presentation{Astronomical Research Using 2MASS Data}{2MASS Research Poster Session}{2008}{Presented results from combining 2MASS $JHK$ photometry with IRTF/SpeX observations to characterize candidate members of the young Galactic cluster [BDS2003] 107, including its stellar population, extinction, distance, age, and approximate mass.}
\presentation{A Novel Method for Low-Toxic Photo-Etch Printing}{Ohio University Research Poster Session}{2004}{Presented Provost's Undergraduate Research Fund-supported work adapting a wintergreen-oil toner-transfer process from lithography to intaglio photo-etch printing, including a low-cost copper- and zinc-plate workflow that replaced xylene- and acetone-based transfer steps and eliminated specialized exposure equipment.}
